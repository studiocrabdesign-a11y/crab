In our studio, light comes before form. Before a wall is drawn, we ask where the sun will enter, at what hour, and what it will fall across.

Concrete rewards this thinking. A raking slice of morning light across a matte surface does more for a room than any applied finish. The material stays honest; the light does the work.

This is the first in a short series on the elements we design with. Next: the weight of shadow.
