Shadow is not the absence of design — it is design. A deep recess, a shaded threshold, a corner left unlit: these give a space its sense of depth and calm.

We plan shadow deliberately, mapping it through the day the same way we map light. The result is interiors that feel settled rather than staged.

Restraint is the discipline. The hardest instruction we give ourselves is to leave a surface alone.
