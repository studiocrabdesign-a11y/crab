---
Every material carries a truth. Concrete wants to read as mass. Timber wants to warm and age. When we hide those truths under coatings, a building loses its voice.

Our detailing aims to expose, not conceal — a formwork seam, the grain of a board, the patina steel earns outdoors.

Buildings made this way improve with age. That, to us, is the point.
