How interior design cost is calculated in India

Interior fit-out cost is usually quoted per square foot of carpet area, and scales with three things: how much of the space is being touched (a partial refresh vs. a full gut renovation vs. fitting out a bare shell), which city you're in (metro rates run higher on both labour and material), and the finish level — modular, factory-made pieces cost far less than custom joinery in solid wood or stone.

The ranges in this calculator are industry ballparks, not a CRAB quote. Two projects at the identical square footage can differ by 2-3x once you factor in structural changes, appliance grade, and how much is custom-built versus off-the-shelf.

How much does interior design cost per square foot in India?

Roughly: essential/basic fit-outs run ₹1,200-1,800/sq ft, mid-range refined interiors ₹1,800-3,000/sq ft, and premium bespoke work ₹3,000-5,000+/sq ft. Rates vary by city, vendor and materials.

What affects the cost the most?

Finish level has the biggest impact, followed by city, followed by project type — new build vs. full renovation vs. partial renovation.

Is this calculator an official quote?

No — it's an illustrative estimate based on typical market ranges, not a quote from CRAB Design Studio. Site conditions and material choices can move the real number significantly.
