How this checker works

Vastu Shastra is a traditional Indian system of architecture that assigns ideal compass directions to different rooms and functions in a home. This checker asks about five of the most commonly discussed placements — main entrance, kitchen, master bedroom, toilet, and pooja/prayer room — and compares your answers against widely published traditional guidance.

It's necessarily a simplification. A full Vastu consultation looks at plot shape, the position of the site itself, structural elements, and often a client's specific horoscope considerations — none of which a five-question web tool can account for. Treat this as a starting point for a conversation, not a final verdict.

Which direction is best for a main entrance?

North, East and Northeast are traditionally considered the most favourable directions for a home's main entrance.

Which direction is best for a kitchen?

Southeast is traditionally considered ideal — sometimes called the "Agni" or fire corner. Northwest is a commonly used secondary option.

Where should the master bedroom be placed?

Southwest is traditionally recommended for the master bedroom, particularly for the head of the household.

Is this checker a substitute for a Vastu consultant?

No. It offers general traditional guidance based on commonly published principles. For a full consultation tailored to your specific plot and structure, speak with a qualified Vastu expert.
